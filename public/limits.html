Limits and pricing

Published plainly, before any upgrade exists. The free plan is what the product enforces today.

Free plan available now

  • 3 stored clips per account. Deleting one frees the slot immediately.
  • Each clip up to 5 minutes long — the recorder stops you at the cap and saves what you have.
  • Each clip up to 100 MB.
  • Share links that play for anyone, no account needed to watch.
  • Download of the original file, export of everything as one archive, and deletion — of one clip or all of them — whenever you want.

Free costs nothing and needs no card.

Paid plan planned — not available yet

$10 / month

  • 2 GB of stored clips.
  • Payments will run in test USDC at first, clearly labelled as tests in the product, 30 days per payment.

There is no upgrade button yet. When payments exist, this page says so.

Your data

  • Your clip list is private to your account.
  • You can export everything (clips plus their metadata) as one archive at any time.
  • Deleting a clip, all clips, or your whole account removes the data for real — share links stop working.
